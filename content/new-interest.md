Well, apparently my last post made my introspect and see what's what with my interest in programming. It made me realise how I had no interest in the programming that I used to and needed to find something new to fuel my curiosity. This led me to a video I found a few years ago by Andrej Karpathy where I made a small [MLP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multilayer_perceptron) and it was actually very interesting. I ran that video back again and I think I have found a new interest in low-level machine learning computing. It combines my two-favorite things about computers (and cars as well) – speed & power. I really like the fact that I get to work with computers at a low-level and extracting every last FLOP to generate cool stuff. I went through other videos that he wher he implements Bigram Models, Transformers, etc. and it has been a REALLY interesting experience. I am super excited to follow through the GPT-2 implementation video and I already have some articles and research papers added to my reading list. I think its safe to say that I found my new interest and I am glad that writing my post gave me back my joy for something that I quite missed.
