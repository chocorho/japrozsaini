It has been approximately a year since my very last post on this website and I wish I had something to show for it but unfortunately I do not. Within these last 12 months or so, my interest in computers or programming in general has dwindled and I have found other new interests which I spend more of my time on. All this is not to say that I don't still love programming, I do (maybe) but I should've anticipated this burnout given that I used to spend 5-6 hours writing code daily. I still go on [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com) every now and then to see what's happening in the world of tech and what's new with the current-gen of AI & ML startups. Some of them seem intriguing but most of them just feel like recycled trash of the same base services slapped on top of market LLMS. Learning Spanish has been going well (KLK) but I have started to spend more time improving my [Punjabi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjabi_language) by reading old literature. I see a lot of cool stuff on linkedin about projects by people at my university and some cool organisations on campus ([ProGSU](https://www.progsu.com)). I want to build new apps, startups or services but don't know where to start. Maybe when I finish writing this post I will find THE idea that I have been looking for. Until then, Goodbye!
